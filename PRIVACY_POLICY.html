Privacy Policy

App: Pen Fight: Last Bench Game

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

100% Privacy-Friendly • No Personal Data Stored

1. Overview

We respect your privacy. Pen Fight: Last Bench Game is designed to be a standalone, nostalgic casual game. We believe games should be played without invasive tracking, surveillance, or personal data collection.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Information: We do NOT collect, sell, transmit, or share your name, phone number, email address, physical location, or contact list.
  • Device Hardware / Vibrator: The game uses device haptic hardware exclusively for in-game tactile feedback (simulating pen collision and desk flicks). No hardware IDs are recorded.
  • Local Storage: Your game progress (unlocked pens, high scores, campaign bench progress) is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your game state to external analytics servers.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences and adheres to child privacy standards (including COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

The app contains zero third-party advertising tracking SDKs. Any external links (such as sharing a duel room link) open in your default browser under your standard browser privacy settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via email: support@lastbench.penfight
